Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 16, 2026

1. Overview

Data Quality Checker is designed with strict data minimization principles. We respect your privacy and process uploaded files strictly to perform data quality checks, previews, and user-directed data cleaning.

2. Uploaded Dataset Handling

- Voluntary Uploads: You choose which CSV files to upload. Files are held in temporary memory during your analysis session.
- No Permanent Storage: Uploaded CSV datasets and cleaned outputs are not permanently stored on disk and are never sold, indexed, or shared with third parties.
- Automatic Expiration: Active sessions expire automatically after 2 hours, purging the working dataset from memory.

3. Log & Technical Information

To protect the service against denial of service and abuse, standard server access logs (such as IP addresses and request timestamps) are retained temporarily for security auditing. Server logs do not record the contents of your CSV records.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

The application uses browser localStorage solely to remember your Dark/Light theme appearance preference. No third-party tracking cookies or advertising pixels are used.

5. Contact

For questions regarding this policy or data handling practices, contact: security@dataqualitychecker.local
